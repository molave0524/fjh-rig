== Power and Interconnect Cabling

This section covers three distinct cabling roles in the rig, each with different requirements:

. **Mains AC power** — 220 VAC L/N/Earth between enclosure inlet, CCPF-1700, and chassis earth. Requires a proper 3-conductor power cable.
. **Signal cabling** — V_PROG, V_MON, INHIBIT, EOC, gate drive. Low-voltage, low-current, but needs shielding against the kA discharge EMI.
. **Discharge loop** — bank to SCR to reactor electrodes. Carries the pulse current. Bus bars by default; flexible options documented for the SCR-to-reactor segment where mechanical compliance is needed.

=== Mains AC power cabling

* *Cable*: 14 AWG, 3-conductor SOOW or SJOOW (e.g., Carol C202814 or equivalent), rated 600 V / 90 °C, EPDM jacket
* *3 × 2 m runs* (revised — previously misspecified as Belden 8267 coax, which is a 2-conductor cable and cannot carry L/N/Earth):
. Run 1: Enclosure AC inlet → CCPF-1700 AC input (L, N, Earth)
. Run 2: CCPF chassis ground stud → rig chassis ground star point (single conductor, 10 AWG green/yellow grounding wire)
. Run 3: E-stop circuit loop (low-voltage signal pair, shielded — does *not* need power cable construction)
* *Terminations*: factory or field-installed strain-relief connectors at both ends; IEC C13/C14 at the CCPF if appropriate
* *Earth bonding*: single-point star ground at the enclosure frame
* *Code compliance*: install per NEC 400.7 for portable cordage in a fixed enclosure context

=== Signal cabling

* STP (shielded twisted pair) with ferrite clamps for V_PROG / V_MON / INHIBIT / EOC
* Twisted pair for gate drive
* Shield grounded *single-point* at the controller (avoid ground loops)

=== Discharge loop (default)

The complete discharge path runs Bank → SCR → reactor. Two segments, both copper bus bar by default. Cable substitution is supported only for the SCR-to-reactor segment (see "flex link alternative" below).

[cols="1,2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Segment | Geometry | Source / spec

| Bank → SCR (cap bank rails) | 25 × 6 mm copper, two rails, ~80 cm length each, SCR mounted at geometric center of 7×2 cap grid for balanced loop inductance | Locked. See `spec/02_capacitor_bank.adoc` (`#sec-cap-bank`)
| SCR → reactor (load segment) | Wide flat copper bus bar or heavy braided strap; specific cross-section / length TBD | **Pending** — `GAP-DISCHARGE-LOOP-GEOMETRY` in `data/pending.yaml`. Default assumption: ≥25 × 6 mm Cu equivalent cross-section to match bank rails; length minimized to keep loop inductance low.
|===

NOTE: The cap-bank-end rails are well-specified; the SCR-to-reactor segment depends on final mechanical layout of the reaction chamber relative to the SCR fixture (see `spec/03_discharge_switch.adoc` and `mechanical/scr-fixture/` when populated). No cable substitution by default — bus bar throughout.

=== Discharge loop (flex link alternative — SCR to reactor only)

When mechanical compliance to a removable reactor assembly is needed, the SCR-to-reactor segment may use a flexible cable. The default Belden 8267 (RG-213/U) is acceptable for short runs with active monitoring; better options exist for sustained or long-run installations.

==== Option: RG-213 (Belden 8267) as discharge flex link

Acceptable for short flex runs with mechanical compliance to a removable reactor assembly. Not a long-term low-maintenance choice; needs scheduled inspection.

* *Length*: ≤ 1 m (0.5 m preferred). Resistive loss is ~1.8% of shot energy per meter at design conditions; per-meter ΔT is ~17 K per shot. Going beyond ~1 m stresses the PVC jacket (rated 80 °C) under repeated shots.
* *Terminations*: crimped lugs only, no solder (solder joints fatigue under repeated pulse heating). Bond braid circumferentially to the return path at both ends; do not pigtail the braid to a single point.
* *Replacement schedule*: every 10,000–20,000 shots, or sooner if inspection signals trigger. Sync with capacitor service intervals where possible.
* *Inspection protocol*:
** Visual check of terminations every ~500 shots — discoloration, jacket damage near lugs
** IR thermometer reading of jacket ~30 s after a shot — track trend, not absolute
** End-to-end DC resistance with milliohmmeter every cap swap — log baseline; 50% increase = replace
** Immediate replacement on: audible snap during discharge, visible jacket damage, resistance > 2× baseline

==== Upgrade paths

Listed in order of incremental improvement over RG-213. The bigger conductor cross-sections and proper pulse-rated constructions remove the inspection-heavy maintenance pattern.

[cols="2,1,1,1,1", options="header"]
|===
| Cable | Conductor area | V rating | Cost (5 m pair) | Expected life

| RG-213 (Belden 8267)        | 5.3 mm²  | 3.7 kV | ~$30   | 10–20k shots
| RG-218/U                     | ~21 mm²  | 5 kV   | ~$150  | 30–50k shots
| Type W 2/0 portable power    | 67 mm²   | 2 kV   | ~$200  | 100k+ shots
| Spot welder kickless cable   | 107+ mm² | 2–5 kV | ~$500+ | 1M+ shots
|===

* *RG-218/U*: incremental upgrade — same coaxial geometry, scaled-up conductor and dielectric.
* *Type W 2/0 portable power*: workhorse upgrade — heavily stranded portable power cable, EPDM jacket, industrial robust. Run two conductors twisted or zip-tied at ~100 mm intervals to keep the inductive loop area small. Terminate with hydraulic-crimped compression lugs.
* *Spot welder kickless cable*: industrial-pedigree option — coaxial geometry built in, designed for millions of pulse cycles. Suppliers: Watteredge, NWL Capacitors, Mersen, Hi-Tech Industries. Water-cooled variants available for sustained high-rate operation.
* For sustained operation > 10 shots/min or runs > 2 m, Type W or kickless cable is recommended over scaling RG-213.

NOTE: Earlier revisions of this spec listed Belden 8267 as the mains AC cable across all three runs. This was incorrect — RG-213/U is a 2-conductor coaxial cable and cannot safely carry L/N/Earth on a single run. The mains AC cable has been revised to proper 3-conductor SOOW. Belden 8267 remains specified only for the discharge flex link (SCR to reactor) where coaxial geometry is appropriate.
