== Diagnostics and Instrumentation [GAP-FILL]

CAUTION: This section was identified as a gap in the original spec. Without diagnostics, there is no way to verify that a shot was electrically nominal.

=== Discharge waveform monitoring

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Instrument | Purpose / spec

| Rogowski coil          | Discharge current waveform, kA-scale (0–10 kA range, ~10 ns rise time, e.g., PEM CWT or similar)
| HV divider             | Bank voltage trace through discharge (10:1 or 100:1, BNC out)
| Oscilloscope           | Capture both traces; 4-channel, 100 MHz minimum, deep memory for full discharge tail
| Photodiode (BPW34, ~$1) | Independent flash t=0 reference; useful sanity check vs. electrical trigger
|===

=== Per-shot data log

ESP32 logs to non-volatile storage on every shot. Schema in `data/shot_log_template.csv`. Fields:

* Shot number (monotonic counter)
* Timestamp
* V_cap_at_fire (V_MON sample at FIRE event)
* V_cap_residual (V_MON after dump-bleed)
* R_sample_pre (4-wire reading before fire)
* R_sample_post (after dump-bleed and probe reconnect)
* Calculated delivered energy (½C(V_initial² - V_final²))
* Atmosphere mode (argon-flush / 7.6 Torr / 10 mTorr)
* Compression force (load-cell at fire)
* Argon flow rate (during purge and fire)
* Fault flags (any FAULT state entered during cycle)
