=== Known Confabulation History

These items were caught and corrected during specification development; documented here to prevent regression:

* "Coilcraft VPH4-0190" — *part does not exist*. Correct part: *SD250-3L*.
* 80 µs gate width — *saturates SD250-3L core*. Corrected to 25–40 µs.
* "ChemGlass 4100N32" quartz tube — *part not in ChemGlass catalog*. Actual source: *Wale Apparatus CFQ18X21.6*.
* Cap-bank ESR figures cited various unsourced per-cap values (~10 mΩ in early spec, ~24 mΩ in audit response). *Correct value: 13 mΩ at 100 Hz, 20 °C* per Kemet ALS70A183QS400 datasheet (confirmed via Digikey listing). Bank ESR (14-cap parallel) = 0.93 mΩ at datasheet conditions; ESR drops further at elevated operating temperature.
