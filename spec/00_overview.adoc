== Purpose and Headline Performance

A bench-scale FJH rig targeted at research-grade material quality with operational tempo suitable for production characterization runs.

[cols="1,2", options="header"]
|===
| Parameter | Target

| Feedstock per shot         | 1 g (headroom to 1.4–2 g)
| Bank stored energy         | ~20 kJ
| Bank delivered energy      | ~14 kJ at 70% efficiency
| Specific energy at 1 g     | ~14 kJ/g (2× the 7 kJ/g threshold)
| Cycle time                 | ~25–30 s (charge-branch limited)
| Operating atmosphere       | Argon flush (1 atm) or 7.6 Torr / 10 mTorr vacuum
| Reactor tube               | 18 mm ID quartz (baseline); BN as upgrade path
|===

.Why 14 caps over the previous 10-cap configuration
[%collapsible]
====
* 40% more delivered energy at the same operating voltage (10 kJ → 14 kJ)
* 2× the threshold-energy margin per gram (1.4× → 2× safety factor)
* Headroom to flash up to ~2 g per shot without rebuilding the bank
* Per-cap pulse-current loading drops by ~30%, easing electrolyte heating
* Trade-off: charge time roughly doubles to ~20 s; the *charge branch is now the cycle-time gate* rather than the sample branch
====
