== Discharge Switch: WESTCODE N170CH12 Press-Pack Thyristor

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Spec | Value

| Package          | W8 (19 mm diameter puck, 70 g)
| Voltage          | 1200 V
| Average current  | 339 A
| Pulse current (ITSM) | 4200 A
| I²t              | 88.2 × 10³ A²s
| V_T0_            | 1.080 V
| r_T_             | 1.300 mΩ
| R_thJK_          | 0.095 K/W (180° sine)
|===

=== Clamping fixture

* Aluminum/copper plates min 6 mm thick
* 4× steel rods
* *PEEK sleeves + shoulder washers* (electrical isolation between rods and plates)
* Guide fixture for 19 mm puck centering
* Belleville washers at 1.5 mm deflection → 10–20 kN clamping force
* Verify contact uniformity post-assembly

=== Gate driver

[source]
----
ESP32 GPIO → opto-isolated MOSFET → Coilcraft SD250-3L → SCR gate
----

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Parameter | Value

| Gate pulse amplitude | 5–10 V peak, 1–1.5 A
| Rise time            | <1 µs
| Pulse width          | ~25–40 µs (hardware timer; *not* 80 µs — saturates SD250-3L)
| Snubber              | R-C across SCR
| Gate routing         | Twisted pair, away from bus bar
|===

NOTE: Earlier specs referenced "Coilcraft VPH4-0190" — *this part does not exist*. Correct part is *SD250-3L*. Earlier specs also called for 80 µs gate width — this saturates the SD250-3L core; corrected to 25–40 µs.
