== Procedures [GAP-FILL]

CAUTION: Procedure sections are placeholder skeletons. Each requires further development before commissioning.

=== First-light commissioning (skeleton)

. Insulation / hipot test of bank with caps disconnected
. Cap reformation procedure (manufacturer spec) if caps stored >6 months
. Bank charge to *50 V only* (5% of operating voltage), verify V_MON reading and bleed
. Bank charge to 100 V, dump-bleed test, verify time constant
. Bank charge to 200 V, fire into *graphite powder* (known-good test feedstock) at low compression
. Verify Rogowski + divider waveforms match predicted (peak current, ringing, decay)
. Step up in 50 V increments to operating point (400 V)
